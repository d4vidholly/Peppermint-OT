About

Rebecca is a Perth-based occupational therapist with 5+ years of clinical experience supporting children and young people across neurodevelopmental, intellectual, and psychosocial disabilities. She brings a trauma-informed, strengths-based approach to every engagement.

  • Specialist clinical background: Experience across community, hospital, and secure care settings — including complex presentations and dual diagnoses.
  • Psychosocial expertise: Skilled in supporting participants with psychosocial disabilities, with a focus on meaningful function and recovery-oriented practice.
  • NDIS assessment knowledge: Deep understanding of NDIS pathways, funding types, and what makes a functional capacity assessment genuinely useful for planners and support coordinators.

Rebecca holds full AHPRA Registration and maintains ongoing professional development to stay current with evidence-based practice.

How It Works

A straightforward process from first contact to final report.

  1. 1

    Initial Consultation

    Book a free initial call to make sure we're the right fit. If we're not, we'll do our best to point you in the right direction.

  2. 2

    Assessment

    Conducted via telehealth or in person where suitable. Sessions are relaxed, strengths-based, and tailored to each individual.

  3. 3

    Report Delivery

    Functional Capacity Assessment reports delivered within 10 business days of assessment — detailed, NDIS-ready, and written with support coordinators in mind.

Plan Managed and Self Managed participants only. PeppermintOT is an unregistered NDIS provider. Services are charged at $193.99/hr in line with the NDIS Price Guide.
